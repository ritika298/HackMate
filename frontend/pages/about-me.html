A Little About Me

I'm a B.Tech student at Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW), currently in my second year. Web development is something I'm exploring one project at a time, and I've found that I learn best by actually building things instead of only reading about them.

I like starting with an idea, figuring out how to make it work, getting stuck, fixing it and trying again. Every project teaches me something new, and that's what keeps me curious.

I'm interested in creating useful digital products, things that make someone's day a little easier. I'm still learning, still experimenting, and enjoying the process.

The Idea Behind HackMate

I wanted to create a platform where students can find teammates, discover hackathons and turn their ideas into real projects.

As a student, I saw how a good idea can stay just an idea when you don't know who to build it with, or where to take it next. Finding people with the right skills, and finding the right opportunities, shouldn't be that hard.

So I built HackMate to bring those pieces together: students, skills, projects and opportunities, all in one place.
